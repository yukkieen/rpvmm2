University Technology Transfer
Descrição: This research deals with the TTU process, which involves several variables and institutions, which should seek ways of managing each environment to achieve the objectives. This approach is based on the "Contingency Model of Effectiveness" where the effectiveness of the TTU can be determined by five dimensions: characteristics of the transfer agent, characteristics of the transfer medium, characteristics of the transfer object, the demand environment and characteristics of the recipient of the transfer. The TTU's success can have several meanings such as impacts on economic development, market impacts, impacts on the personnel involved, political impact and social impact. In this sense, the study aims to contribute to the academic literature by grouping these critical factors into effectiveness criteria based on the "Revised Contingency Model of Effectiveness". A second, objective is validating by field exploratory research the initial part of this research started in Brazil. Also, it is expected to find out alternatives for a broader evaluation of the TTU process that can be modeled. That is after the critical factors are established as determinants of technology transfer effectiveness it is possible to optimize strategic plans of action. Besides, the characterization of universities according to the criteria of efficiency may enable the development of compatible incentive systems for the involvement of scientists who also play academic roles..

University Technology Transfer
Descrição: This research deals with the TTU process, which involves several variables and institutions, which should seek ways of managing each environment to achieve the objectives. This approach is based on the "Contingency Model of Effectiveness" where the effectiveness of the TTU can be determined by five dimensions: characteristics of the transfer agent, characteristics of the transfer medium, characteristics of the transfer object, the demand environment and characteristics of the recipient of the transfer. The TTU's success can have several meanings such as impacts on economic development, market impacts, impacts on the personnel involved, political impact and social impact. In this sense, the study aims to contribute to the academic literature by grouping these critical factors into effectiveness criteria based on the "Revised Contingency Model of Effectiveness". A second, objective is validating by field exploratory research the initial part of this research started in Brazil. Also, it is expected to find out alternatives for a broader evaluation of the TTU process that can be modeled. That is after the critical factors are established as determinants of technology transfer effectiveness it is possible to optimize strategic plans of action. Besides, the characterization of universities according to the criteria of efficiency may enable the development of compatible incentive systems for the involvement of scientists who also play academic roles..
